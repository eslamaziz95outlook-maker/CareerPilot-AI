# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in CareerPilot AI a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone regardless of background, identity, experience level, or location.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Critique ideas and code rather than people. Respect privacy, especially when examples involve career documents or personal information. Do not submit real third-party personal data to issues or pull requests without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, or publishing another person's sensitive information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and interactions that violate these expectations, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

## Scope

This code applies in repository spaces and when representing the project in public community spaces.
